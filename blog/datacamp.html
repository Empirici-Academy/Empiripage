DataCamp Scholarship

Access to DataCamp's learning platform for motivated learners.

Empirici Academy has partnered with DataCamp Donates to provide motivated learners with access to DataCamp's learning platform. Build job-ready skills in data science, analytics, and AI.

What's Included

DataCamp Learning Access

Access DataCamp's courses, career tracks, skill assessments, and hands-on learning resources across data, technology, and AI.

Hands-On Projects

Interactive coding exercises and real-world projects to build practical, job-ready skills.

Certifications

Earn DataCamp certificates for eligible completed courses and tracks that you can add to your professional portfolio.

Community Support

Connect with other learners through Empirici Academy's learning community and continue developing practical technical skills, together.

Eligibility

Students

Currently enrolled university students and recent graduates seeking to develop practical data and technical skills.

Career Changers

Professionals and learners transitioning into data, technology, or related technical roles.

Self-Learners

Motivated individuals building technical skills independently and committed to consistent learning.

Requirements: Commitment to actively using the platform, sufficient English proficiency to follow the learning materials, and access to a computer with internet connection.

How to Apply

1

Complete the Application

Fill out the online application with your background, learning goals, and motivation for joining the scholarship program.

2

Wait for Selection

We receive a large number of applications each month, while the number of available scholarships is limited. Applications are reviewed as new scholarship spots become available, so applying does not guarantee immediate selection.

3

Receive Your Invitation

When scholarship spots become available, selected applicants receive an invitation to join the program. New invitations are sent monthly based on available spots.

4

Stay Active

Once accepted, use your DataCamp access and complete the required monthly assignment to remain active in the scholarship program.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the scholarship last?

Access is provided to active scholarship recipients. Recipients are expected to complete the required monthly assignment to maintain their access.

Is there a cost to apply?

No. Applying for the scholarship is completely free, and selected recipients do not pay Empirici for their DataCamp access.

Who can apply?

The scholarship is open to learners in the wider technical and data community. You do not need to be an existing Empirici student or have previously purchased one of our courses.

How do I keep my scholarship active?

Scholarship recipients are expected to remain engaged with their learning and complete the required monthly assignment. Continued access depends on active participation.

What happens if I don't complete the monthly assignment?

Access may be discontinued if the required monthly assignment is not completed. This allows us to make scholarship opportunities available to learners who are actively using them.

What happens if I am not selected right away?

Due to limited spots per batch, we can't accept everyone immediately. However, we keep your application on file and will review it for upcoming monthly bacth as new spots open up.

Do I need prior coding or data experience?

No prior experience is required. DataCamp offers learning paths and courses for different skill levels, from beginners to advanced learners.

What does the scholarship include?

Selected recipients receive access to DataCamp through our scholarship program. The available learning resources depend on the access provided through the DataCamp Donates program.

Can I apply if I am already an Empirici student?

Yes. However, the scholarship is not limited to Empirici students and is also available to eligible learners from the wider technical community.

Can I apply if I cannot afford paid courses?

Yes. Our scholarship initiatives are designed to help reduce financial barriers to technical education and provide learning opportunities to members of the wider community.

Ready to Start Learning?

We receive a high volume of applications every month, and while our scholarship spots are limited, every applicant matters to us. Thank you so much for your patience as we review each profile carefully. Apply now to secure your spot for upcoming monthly selections!
